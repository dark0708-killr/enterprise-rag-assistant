# Apex Global Holdings - Q3 2024 Consolidated Financial & Operational Report
**Document ID:** AGH-FIN-2024-Q3  
**Publication Date:** October 18, 2024  
**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2024 - September 30, 2024  
**Auditor:** Deloitte & Touche LLP  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Highlights
Apex Global Holdings delivered record operational performance in the third quarter of fiscal year 2024, driven by accelerated enterprise adoption of our flagship AI-driven workflow platform and resilient demand across multi-cloud infrastructure services.

* **Consolidated Total Revenue:** $428.5 million, an increase of 24.2% year-over-year (YoY) compared to $345.0 million in Q3 2023.
* **GAAP Gross Profit:** $312.8 million (73.0% gross margin), expanding by 280 basis points YoY.
* **Operating Income (GAAP):** $78.9 million (18.4% operating margin), compared to $49.2 million (14.3% operating margin) in Q3 2023.
* **Non-GAAP Diluted Net Income Per Share:** $1.18, exceeding consensus estimates of $1.04.
* **Operating Cash Flow:** $106.4 million; **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** $84.2 million (19.6% FCF margin).
* **Cash, Equivalents & Marketable Securities:** $842.6 million with zero long-term debt maturities until 2029.

---

## 2. Business Segment Financial Performance

### 2.1 Enterprise AI SaaS Solutions
* **Revenue:** $184.2 million (43.0% of total revenue), up 38.6% YoY.
* **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** $712.0 million at quarter-end.
* **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** 128% across enterprise accounts (>1,000 employees).
* **Enterprise Customers:** 1,842 organizations with >$100,000 in ARR, up 22% YoY.

### 2.2 Cloud Infrastructure & Managed Services
* **Revenue:** $142.1 million (33.2% of total revenue), up 15.4% YoY.
* **Gross Margin:** 68.2%, reflecting optimization of multi-region GPU clusters.
* **Customer Uptime Delivered:** 99.992%, exceeding contractual 99.99% service level commitments.

### 2.3 Cybersecurity & Compliance Solutions
* **Revenue:** $102.2 million (23.8% of total revenue), up 16.1% YoY.
* **Key Growth Driver:** Influx of automated FedRAMP High and EU NIS2 automated compliance onboarding.

---

## 3. Operating Expenses Breakdown
* **Research & Development (R&D):** $98.4 million (23.0% of revenue), focused on proprietary quantized LLM inference engines and synthetic data synthesis.
* **Sales & Marketing (S&M):** $102.6 million (23.9% of revenue), demonstrating continuing leverage from automated inbound product-led growth (PLG).
* **General & Administrative (G&A):** $32.9 million (7.7% of revenue).

---

## 4. Guidance & Outlook for Q4 2024 and Full-Year 2024
For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, management projects:
* **Consolidated Revenue:** $460.0 million to $475.0 million.
* **GAAP Operating Margin:** 18.5% to 19.5%.
* **Full-Year 2024 Total Revenue:** Expected to reach $1,680 million to $1,700 million (representing ~23% YoY growth).
* **CapEx Projections:** Approximately $35.0 million for data center liquid cooling retrofits.
